# Policy classification wording

The report currently combines valid NEUTRAL evidence and unavailable evidence in one label. Display Policy stance: Neutral (relative to inflation and r-star) for NEUTRAL, and Policy stance: Unavailable for missing or invalid states. Apply the same distinction to neutral versus unavailable reserve liquidity. Preserve restrictive and accommodative classifications and existing quality gates.

Show the nominal effective federal funds rate as a percentage alongside the stance, with its observation date. Explain that policy stance uses nominal DFF minus core PCE inflation minus r-star, with a neutral band of ±0.50 percentage points. High nominal rates and a neutral estimated real-policy gap can coexist. Do not classify nominal rates as high without an explicit historical benchmark.

Use specific gating reasons already supplied by the analysis rather than wording that implies all gated quadrants have missing data. Verify valid neutral inputs, missing inputs, restrictive inputs, and independently gated valid states. Regenerate daily and weekly public artifacts and verify the live panels after deployment.
